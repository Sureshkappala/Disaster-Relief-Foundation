About the Foundation

Driven by transparency and trust, we bridge the gap between global donors and disaster-affected communities.

Our Founding & Evolution

Established in 2018 following a series of devastating cyclones, Stackly (Disaster Relief Foundation) was formed by a team of rescue coordinators, supply chain experts, and medical reserves who saw the critical need for a faster, logistics-centric response system.

What started as a small volunteer network has grown into a structured, internationally recognized non-governmental organization. We combine state-of-the-art dispatch protocols with deep-rooted community partnerships to ensure that aid reaches victims when they need it most—without bureaucratic delay.

Our Strategic Objective

At Stackly, our primary objective is to minimize human suffering during natural crises. We specialize in building local community networks, equipping them with water systems and rescue training, and establishing emergency storage cells containing survival goods.

Through transparency and clear audits, we show our donors exactly where their contributions are used. By utilizing Web3 reporting and live dispatch trackers, we provide unprecedented transparency in disaster funding.

Our Mission

Immediate Humanitarian Action

To mobilize trained rescue coordinators, distribution reserves, and essential resources to disaster-hit zones within hours, saving lives and providing the foundation for sustainable recovery.

Our Vision

Resilient Communities

A world where every community vulnerable to natural hazards possesses the training, supplies, and rapid support network to withstand crisis, preserve human life, and rebuild with dignity.

Humanitarian Values

The Principles that Drive Us

We operate under a strict code of ethics in compliance with international disaster response frameworks.

Impartiality & Respect

Aid is delivered based on critical human need alone, regardless of race, creed, gender, or political alignment.

Rapid Readiness

We maintain disaster reserves, standby volunteers, and boat squads to ensure instantaneous mobilization.

Total Auditability

We publish third-party audited financial records for every disaster relief project we coordinate.

Strategic Deployment

The Logistics of Rescue

We believe that effective disaster response is built on advanced logistics planning rather than simple ad-hoc volunteering. Our approach focuses on:

  • Hotspot Mapping: Tracking local rivers and fault lines to pre-position resources.
  • Base Camp Hubs: Setting up command centers with satellite communication grids.
  • Partner Integration: Interfacing directly with local hospitals and safety councils.
  • Rehab Auditing: Following up post-crisis to measure the structural recovery of shelters.
